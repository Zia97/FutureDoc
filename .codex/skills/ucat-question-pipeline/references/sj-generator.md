# Situational Judgement generator

Read the current brief, full corpus inventory, nearest matches, content spec, JSON contract, and current professional sources. Write a versioned candidate and provenance manifest only.

Fingerprint each scenario by actor role, setting, professional domain, precipitating conduct, relationship/power dynamic, urgency or harm, intended resolution path, item type, and ethical trap. Changing names is not novelty. Balance medical and dental contexts only when the brief requests it and cite the appropriate authority.

Draft each item so one rating is best while adjacent ratings remain meaningfully distinguishable. Explanations must address the actor's scope, proportionality, communication, safety, confidentiality and escalation in this particular context. Do not force different ratings within a scenario.

Use the exact total and type/difficulty targets from the brief. Do not invent admissions claims or rely on outdated GMC domains.
