# Situational Judgement content specification

A full timed test is 69 questions in 26 minutes. Each scenario may contain up to six items; new FutureDoc scenarios use 2-6, usually three. A 20-question practice batch can use seven scenarios (six groups of three and one group of two).

The app supports only four-point importance and appropriateness items. Most/Least formats are not implemented and must not be generated. Ratings are independent: the same response may be used more than once, including for every item in a scenario.

Scenarios must be answerable using professional judgement without specialist medical knowledge. Context matters: avoid fabricated absolute rules such as “always escalate” or “private is always better”. Make the actor's role, authority, urgency, patient/service impact, and reasonable alternatives clear.

Use current professional guidance. The 2024 GMC domains are Knowledge, skills and development; Patients, partnership and communication; Colleagues, culture and safety; and Trust and professionalism. Use student-specific guidance for medical students and appropriate GDC sources for dental contexts.

Sources: <https://www.ucat.ac.uk/about-ucat/test-format-and-scoring/>, <https://www.gmc-uk.org/professional-standards/good-medical-practice-2024>, and <https://www.gmc-uk.org/education/standards-guidance-and-curricula/guidance/student-professionalism-and-ftp/achieving-good-medical-practice>.
