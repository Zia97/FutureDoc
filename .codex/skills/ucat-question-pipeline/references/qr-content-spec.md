# Quantitative Reasoning content specification

A full timed test is 36 questions in 26 minutes. A stimulus set contains 1-4 questions. Practice batches use the exact question total and allocation in their brief; do not force a full-test distribution onto a 20-question batch.

Supported stimulus types are `table`, `bar_chart`, `line_graph`, `pie_chart`, `scatter_plot`, `network_diagram`, `geometry_diagram`, `text`, `formula`, and recursive `multi`. Visual/table stimuli need context. Tables must be rectangular; chart dimensions and series lengths must agree; pie totals, network endpoints, scatter coordinates, and geometry shapes must be internally valid.

Each question has five A-E options and one defensible answer. Put genuinely numeric options in ascending order when units are comparable; categorical and date choices use logical order. Questions within a set must be self-contained rather than relying on an earlier answer.

Explanations show the method, intermediate values, rounding and units, then name the final answer. A formula may be supplied when pedagogically appropriate; do not apply contradictory blanket rules.

Official current section timing/count source: <https://www.ucat.ac.uk/about-ucat/test-format-and-scoring/>.
